📖 How to Play Words Builder

⭐ Starting Word

The very first word of the season must cover the center star tile at coordinates (0, 0).

📏 Straight Lines & No Gaps

Tiles must be placed in a single continuous line (horizontal or vertical). Words can bridge across existing letters, but cannot have empty spaces.

🔗 Crossword Connectivity

Every new play must connect to at least one tile already on the board. All intersecting words formed must also be valid words.

📚 Official Dictionary

All words must exist in the standard SOWPODS dictionary (minimum 2 letters long).

💥 Bonus Squares & Multipliers

  • DL / TL: Double / Triple letter score (fresh tiles only).
  • DW / TW: Double / Triple total word score.
  • Length Multiplier: 5+ letters (1.5x), 7+ letters (2.0x).
  • BINGO Bonus: Use all 7 rack tiles for a +50 point bonus!

🃏 Wildcard (*)

The wildcard tile can represent any letter A-Z. Its base value is 0 points, but it triggers word multipliers!
